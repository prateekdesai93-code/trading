How the multi-timeframe signal is calculated
Not financial advice. This dashboard performs automated technical analysis on live spot prices and is provided for informational and educational purposes only. Technical indicators describe historical price behaviour and do not guarantee future results. Precious metals prices are volatile and can move against any position. Always size positions with independent risk management and, if needed, consult a licensed financial adviser before trading. Live prices come from a free third-party market data endpoint (gold-api.com) and may differ slightly from your broker's quote or be briefly delayed. Every chart and indicator on this page is built only from prices this dashboard has personally observed since it was opened — nothing is backfilled or simulated — so the shortest timeframes (1m, 5m) become reliable first, and the longest (4H, 1D) take proportionally longer; 1-Month and 1-Year views need a paid historical data feed and are intentionally left as an explained empty state rather than faked.
